Haohao Qu

I study how Large Language Models can help us understand and improve Generative Recommendation.

I am currently a PhD candidate of the Department of Computing, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, under the supervision of Prof. Wenqi Fan and Prof. Qing Li. My research interest covers Recommender Systems, Smart Cities, Large Language Models, Agent Reasoning. I have authored and co-authored over 20 innovative papers, most of them have been published in top journals (e.g., IEEE TKDE, IEE TITS, and ACM TOIS) and conferences (e.g., ICLR, WWW, and KDD).

01 / Overview

Representative work

Towards autonomous recommendation agents.

01

ContRec (WWW 2026)

ContRec represents users&items as latent vector representations using a not-quantized tokenizer and leverages the exceptional continuous-valued generation capability of diffusion models to operate within continuous spaces and generate implicit user preferences conditioned on the reasoning content of LLMs. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12007

02

TokenRec (IEEE TKDE 2025)

A LLM-based Recommender System with user&item Tokenizers and a generative retrieval paradigm. TokenRec consists of the masked vector-quantized tokenizer with a K-way encoder for item ID tokenization and the generative retrieval paradigm for recommendation generation. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10450

03

UrbanEV

A real-world dataset (Shenzhen, China) for EV-related research, e.g., spatiotemporal prediction and urban energy management. Repo: https://github.com/IntelligentSystemsLab/ST-EVCDP
